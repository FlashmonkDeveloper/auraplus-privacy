Privacy Policy

Aura+: Private Period Tracker · Effective 2 November 2026

Aura+ keeps your health records on your phone and nowhere else. There are no accounts, no servers, no analytics and no ads, and the app has no internet access. We could not see your records even if we wanted to.

What we collect

Nothing. Aura+ does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal or health data. The app has no sign-in, does not connect to any server, and contains no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting software. It does not request internet permission.

What stays on your phone

Aura+ stores only what you choose to enter, in the app's private storage on your device, for example:

  • Period and bleeding days, symptoms, moods, pain, sleep and energy, notes and journal entries
  • Fertility observations (temperature, cervical mucus, home test results) and optional fertility records
  • Pregnancy and postpartum records, appointments, measurements, feeds and diaper counts
  • Medications and doses, lab results and custom trackers
  • Your settings, such as language, theme, reminders and which health areas are turned on

How it is protected

  • Encrypted on the device. Your records are kept in a database encrypted with AES-256 (SQLCipher). The encryption key is protected by the Android Keystore and never leaves your phone.
  • No cloud copies. Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer are turned off for Aura+, so the system does not copy your records to Google or any other cloud.
  • App lock. If you turn it on, Aura+ asks for your phone's PIN, pattern, password or biometrics before it opens.
  • Discreet notifications. Reminders can be shown without any health details on the lock screen.

Backups and reports you create

  • Backups you export. Vault → Export a backup saves a backup file wherever you choose. You can protect it with a passphrase (AES-256-GCM). A backup without a passphrase is readable by anyone who has the file, so keep it somewhere you trust.
  • Health report. You can save a PDF summary, for example to take to an appointment. Where you keep or share it is up to you.

We never receive a copy of either file.

Permissions and why they are used

  • Notifications: to show the reminders you set.
  • Run at startup: to set your reminders again after the phone restarts.
  • Biometrics (fingerprint): only if you turn on App lock. The check is done entirely by Android; Aura+ never receives or stores your fingerprint.

Aura+ does not use the internet, your location, contacts, camera, microphone or files beyond the backup and report files you choose to save or open.

Google Play services

Aura+ asks the Google Play Store app whether a newer version is available so it can prompt you to update, and the Rate Aura+ button opens the app's Google Play page. These are handled by Google Play under your Google account; Aura+ sends none of your records to Google.

Deleting your data

You can delete individual records inside the app. Uninstalling Aura+ permanently deletes everything it stored on your phone. Backup or report files you exported are separate files that you can delete yourself.

Not medical advice

Aura+ shows estimates and observations based only on what you record. It is not a medical device, it is not a form of contraception, and it does not replace advice from a qualified health professional.

Children

Aura+ is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Your responsibility

Because your data lives only on your device, keeping it private is in your hands. If other people use your phone, turn on App lock in Settings → Privacy and security, and store exported backups somewhere safe.

Changes to this policy

If a future version changes how data is handled, for example if optional paid features are added through Google Play, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
